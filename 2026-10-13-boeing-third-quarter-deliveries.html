Boeing’s third-quarter delivery mix and the fleet plan

Market snapshot as of October 13, 2026
Publication date pending

Boeing announced preliminary third-quarter deliveries of 161 commercial airplanes, including 124 737s and 22 787s. Its October 13 release said the delivery information would be final when quarterly financial results were issued.

The mix is useful context for an airline evaluating capacity across several aircraft families. A fleet team could compare the availability of each type against its own deadlines and commonality requirements. Include cabin work, crew and maintenance readiness, and the timing of any lease returns already on the calendar. That gives the buyer a clearer view of whether each option can cover the capacity gap when it occurs.
