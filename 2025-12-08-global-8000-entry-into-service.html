Bombardier marks the Global 8000’s entry into service

Market snapshot as of December 8, 2025
Publication date pending

Bombardier announced the Global 8000’s entry into service after delivering an aircraft at its assembly center in Mississauga, Ontario. The announcement gave large-cabin buyers a new reference point for comparing an in-service model with other options on their shortlist.

For a buyer weighing a factory position against a used long-range jet, the comparison extends beyond range and cabin layout. Ask for a dated outline of crew training, service-center access and support setup. A used aircraft may have readily available operating history. A factory delivery may give the owner more say in configuration. Putting each advantage against an actual entry-into-use date helps make the choice concrete.
