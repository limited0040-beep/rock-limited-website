Airbus reports 351 deliveries through June 2026

Market snapshot as of July 29, 2026
Publication date pending

Airbus reported 351 commercial aircraft deliveries in the first half of 2026, compared with 306 in the same period a year earlier. The manufacturer also reported 821 net commercial orders for the half year.

For a fleet buyer, deliveries describe aircraft already handed over, while net orders describe additions to the order book after cancellations. Each tells a different part of the supply story. For a specific route launch, compare a documented delivery offer with a lease or used-aircraft alternative. Set the comparison around the month of revenue service and include aircraft induction time in each estimate.
