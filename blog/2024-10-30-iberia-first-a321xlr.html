Iberia takes the first A321XLR

Market snapshot as of October 30, 2024
Publication date pending

Airbus announced that Iberia had accepted the first A321XLR. Iberia planned regional flying before its first transatlantic mission, from Madrid to Boston, later in November.

For a fleet team evaluating a longer-range narrowbody, the handover illustrated the distinction between receiving an aircraft and putting it onto its intended route. A planning comparison could use the actual cabin configuration, expected traffic mix and seasonal payload. It could also allow time between handover and long-haul service for crew readiness, spares and proving the schedule. A route decision rests on those inputs together.
