Executive jet purchases

What 322 business jet shipments meant for a buyer’s search

Market snapshot as of September 9, 2024
Publication date pending

GAMA’s September report counted 322 worldwide business jet shipments in the first half of 2024, with 195 in the second quarter. That gave buyers a view of completed manufacturer shipments through June.

A buyer looking for near-term lift could build a separate list of candidate serial numbers with estimated closing dates, inspection slots and planned maintenance. Ask which aircraft are physically available for inspection and which are still in active use. Putting those dates next to a factory quote helps distinguish the delivery schedule you can plan around from the broad industry figures.
