Airbus ends 2025 with 793 deliveries and a record backlog

Market snapshot as of January 12, 2026
Publication date pending

Airbus reported 793 commercial deliveries in 2025 and an overall year-end backlog of 8,754 aircraft. Of that backlog, 1,124 were widebodies.

An airline evaluating interim lift could use the published figures to frame a conversation about future supply while keeping the decision focused on actual offers. For a widebody, compare handover timing with records review, heavy checks and cabin work before committing the capacity to a route. For a lease extension, compare the extra months of use with upcoming maintenance and return requirements. Those two paths can look quite different once the operator puts them on the same calendar.
