Executive jet purchases

Gulfstream G700 clears a certification milestone

Market snapshot as of March 29, 2024
Publication date pending

The FAA certified Gulfstream’s G700, Reuters reported on March 29. That gave buyers evaluating a new large-cabin jet a concrete milestone to put alongside quoted delivery timing. Individual aircraft still had to move through completion and handover before an owner could plan around them.

A buyer comparing a G700 position with an available aircraft could ask for the delivery window, cabin completion milestones and acceptance process in writing. If the purchase was tied to selling an existing jet, the dates for the two transactions deserved separate schedules. That makes it easier to price interim lift if the handover moves.
