Business jet shipments rise in the first half of 2026

Market snapshot as of September 7, 2026
Publication date pending

GAMA reported 383 business jet shipments through June 2026, up 8.2 percent from the same period in 2025. The industrywide figure gave buyers a reference point for new production across manufacturers.

An acquisition team could pair that report with a shortlist built around its own delivery deadline. For each candidate, confirm when the seller can make it available for inspection, whether the records are ready to review, and which maintenance events are due after closing. An aircraft that can close quickly may still need work before regular use. That difference matters when comparing a factory slot with a used jet.
