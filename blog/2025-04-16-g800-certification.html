Gulfstream G800 earns U.S. and European certification

Market snapshot as of April 16, 2025
Publication date pending

Gulfstream announced FAA and EASA type certification for the G800 on April 16. For buyers comparing long-range aircraft, the milestone made it possible to revisit proposals with more specific questions about the approved aircraft and the configuration being offered.

The most useful comparison starts with the owner’s recurring city pairs. Ask each seller to model the same trip with the same passenger count, baggage and planning assumptions. Then compare what happens when the weather or runway limits are less favorable. A buyer can carry that analysis into discussions about acceptance flights, crew preparation and the time needed to put the jet into regular use.
