Commercial aviation

Airbus midyear deliveries put fleet timing in focus

Market snapshot as of July 30, 2024
Publication date pending

Airbus reported 323 commercial aircraft deliveries in the first half of 2024 and an order backlog of 8,585 at the end of June. It was working through supply-chain constraints while preparing for higher production.

For an airline planning replacement capacity, the useful comparison was between the handover dates available under a purchase, a used-aircraft acquisition and a lease extension. An operator could model how long each option would take to enter service, including inspections, cabin changes and training. The aggregate delivery figure gave context. The decision still depended on the specific aircraft and the months it could actually fly.
