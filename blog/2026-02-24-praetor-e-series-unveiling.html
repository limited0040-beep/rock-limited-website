Embraer unveils the next generation of its Praetor jets

Market snapshot as of February 24, 2026
Publication date pending

Embraer unveiled the Praetor 600E and 500E on February 24, describing them as the first evolution of the Praetor family. The announcement gave a buyer considering a midsize or super-midsize jet a reason to revisit the factory and used-aircraft shortlists.

The useful comparison is between the configuration an owner can buy and the missions it needs to serve. Identify the cabin features that matter on regular trips, then ask which are included on the offered aircraft and which depend on options or completion work. Run the same comparison against a pre-owned Praetor and include the expected handover, training and early maintenance dates.
