Vietjet’s A321neo memorandum puts order status in focus

Market snapshot as of June 17, 2025
Publication date pending

At the Paris Air Show, Airbus announced a memorandum of understanding with Vietjet covering 100 A321neo aircraft, with the potential for 50 more. The announcement described a memorandum at this point in the transaction.

For a fleet planner, the status of a deal determines how it belongs in a capacity forecast. An initial commitment can appear in a planning scenario while the operator continues to confirm contract milestones and delivery windows. Additional aircraft with potential to be added belong in a separate scenario. Treating those groups separately makes it easier to see what happens to lease needs if some capacity comes later.
